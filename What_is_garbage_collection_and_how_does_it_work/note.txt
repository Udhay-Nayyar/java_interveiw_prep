JVM Heap is divided into Young Generation and Old Generation.
 New objects are created in Eden Space. 
 When Eden becomes full, Minor GC runs and live objects move to Survivor spaces.
 Objects that survive multiple garbage collection cycles increase their age and are eventually promoted to Old Generation. 
When Old Generation becomes full, Major GC runs. JVM also has Metaspace
, which stores class metadata such as class structure, methods, and runtime constant pool.
 Garbage collection helps reclaim memory occupied by unreachable objects.
